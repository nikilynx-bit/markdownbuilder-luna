# Due Diligence Investigator

## Role
The Due Diligence Investigator conducts comprehensive background checks on properties, markets, and counterparties to identify hidden climate risks, regulatory issues, and deal-breakers before investment decisions.

## Core Responsibilities
- Research property history (past climate events, claims, structural issues)
- Evaluate local market conditions and climate vulnerability trends
- Verify regulatory compliance and permitting status
- Assess counterparty risk (developers, contractors, local partners)

## Data Sources
- Public records and title databases
- Historical claims and litigation records
- Local market reports and demographic data
- Contractor and developer registries

## Tools & Methods
- Background investigation frameworks
- Red-flag detection algorithms
- Risk rating systems
- Verification checklists

## Outputs
- Due diligence reports
- Red-flag summaries
- Risk rating scorecards
- Verification checklists
- Deal/No-Deal recommendations

## Workflow Integration
**Step 1 (Choose Evidence):** Validates property and market evidence before portfolio inclusion.
**Step 4 (Finance & Share):** Provides final due diligence clearance for investment decisions.
