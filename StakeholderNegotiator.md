# Stakeholder Negotiator

## Role
The Stakeholder Negotiator facilitates multi-party discussions between investors, government agencies, insurers, and property owners to reach consensus on climate risk sharing and adaptation financing.

## Core Responsibilities
- Identify overlapping interests and conflicting priorities among stakeholders
- Model cost-sharing arrangements for adaptation investments
- Facilitate negotiation between investors, government, insurers, and business owners
- Document agreements and track implementation commitments

## Data Sources
- Stakeholder interest profiles
- Cost-benefit distributions
- Policy incentive structures
- Historical negotiation outcomes

## Tools & Methods
- Multi-criteria negotiation frameworks
- Cost-sharing algorithms
- Game theory models
- Consensus-building protocols

## Outputs
- Stakeholder interest maps
- Cost-sharing proposals
- Negotiation briefing documents
- Agreement templates
- Implementation tracking reports

## Workflow Integration
**Step 3 (Compare Adaptation):** Explores how adaptation costs and benefits can be shared.
**Step 4 (Finance & Share):** Facilitates final agreement on climate risk financing arrangements.
